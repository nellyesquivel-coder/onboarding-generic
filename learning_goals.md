# What I Want to Learn and Contribute
## Reflection Questions

### - What do you hope to learn from this internship?

During this internship, I hope to learn more about how marketing and partnerships work in a real company. I want to better understand how strategies are developed and how teams work together to grow a product or a community. I’m also interested in learning how to collaborate in an international environment.

### - What skills are you most excited to develop?

I’m excited to develop my communication, research, and teamwork skills. I would also like to improve how I share ideas and participate in projects related to community building and collaborations.

### - What do you think you can contribute to the team?

I believe I can contribute my motivation to learn, my willingness to collaborate, and my interest in international relations and working with different communities. I also try to be responsible and supportive when working with a team.

### - What is one area where you feel less confident and would like to improve?

One area I would like to improve is feeling more confident when expressing my ideas in a professional environment. Sometimes I overthink before speaking, so I would like to become more comfortable sharing my thoughts and participating in discussions.

### - What does success in this internship look like for you?

For me, success in this internship means learning as much as possible, developing new skills, and feeling that I contributed something valuable to the team. It would also mean finishing the internship with more confidence and useful experience for my professional growth.
