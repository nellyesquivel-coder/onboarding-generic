# Professionalism Reflection

## Research and Learning

### Examples of professional vs. unprofessional behavior in a casual work environment?

In a casual workplace like Focus Bear, being professional doesn’t mean being overly formal. It means treating others with respect, communicating clearly, and being responsible with your work.

Professional behavior can include being prepared for meetings, responding to messages, listening to others, and following through on tasks. Unprofessional behavior could include ignoring messages, coming to meetings without preparation, interrupting others, or not following up on responsibilities.

Even in a relaxed environment, respect and accountability are still important.

### What respectful communication looks like in remote teams?

In remote teams, respectful communication means being clear, polite, and mindful of tone. Since most communication happens through messages, it’s important to write in a way that avoids misunderstandings.

It also helps to assume good intentions, ask questions when something isn’t clear, and handle disagreements calmly. Being patient and respectful helps maintain a positive team environment.

### How to give and receive feedback professionally?

Giving feedback professionally means focusing on the work and not on the person. The goal should always be to help improve the work and support the team.

Receiving feedback means listening carefully and being open to suggestions. Instead of taking feedback personally, it’s better to see it as an opportunity to learn and improve.

## Reflection
### Have you ever experienced great teamwork and professionalism?

Yes, I have experienced good teamwork during university group projects. What made the experience positive was that everyone communicated openly and respected each other’s ideas. When everyone takes responsibility for their part and supports each other, the team works much more smoothly.

### What steps can you take to ensure your communication is clear and respectful?

I can take time to write clear messages and think before sending them. I can also try to listen carefully to others, be respectful of different perspectives, and ask questions when something is unclear.

### How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?

I can contribute by being supportive, respectful, and maintaining a positive attitude. Small actions like acknowledging others’ work or helping teammates when possible can make the work environment more positive.

### How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?

To prepare for meetings, I can review the project or topic in advance, check my progress, and bring any questions or updates. Being prepared helps make meetings more productive and respectful of everyone’s time.

### What does being proactive during your internship look like?

Being proactive means taking initiative instead of waiting to be told what to do. It includes looking for solutions, asking thoughtful questions, and sharing ideas when I think they could help the project.

### How could you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information?

If I need information, I would send a polite message asking for clarification. If I don’t receive a response after some time and the task is important, I would send a follow-up message. If it becomes urgent, I would escalate the communication using another channel.

### What things will you not do?

I will avoid ignoring messages, coming to meetings unprepared, or waiting for instructions without taking initiative. I will also avoid reacting defensively to feedback and instead try to learn from it.
