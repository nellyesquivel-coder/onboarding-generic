# Cyber Security Reflection
## Research and Learning
### *1. What are common cybersecurity threats in a remote work environment?*
Some common cybersecurity threats in remote work include phishing emails, suspicious links, weak passwords, unsecured Wi-Fi networks, and unauthorized access to accounts or devices.

### *2. What are best practices for keeping your devices and accounts secure?*
Some good practices include using strong passwords, enabling two-factor authentication, keeping software updated, locking devices when not in use, and avoiding suspicious links or downloads.

### *3. Why is it important to lock your computer when you step away from your desk?*
Locking the computer prevents other people from accessing company or user information. Even a short moment away from the device could create a security risk if the screen is left open.

### *4. How should you handle phishing attempts and suspicious links?*
If an email or message looks suspicious, it is better not to click on any links or download attachments. Instead, verify the source or report the message to the appropriate team member.

### *5. What makes a password strong and why should you use a password manager?*
A strong password usually includes a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, and it should be unique for each account. A password manager helps store and manage passwords securely without needing to remember all of them.

### *6. Why is two-factor authentication (2FA) important?*
Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security. Even if someone gets access to a password, they would still need a second verification step to log in.

## Reflection 
### *What security measures do you currently follow and where could you improve?*
Currently, I try to use secure passwords and avoid clicking on suspicious links. However, I can improve by being more consistent with security habits like enabling two-factor authentication on all work accounts and organizing my passwords more securely.

### *How can you turn secure behavior into a habit?*
Security can become a habit by following simple routines, such as locking devices when stepping away, checking links before clicking them, and regularly updating passwords.

### *What steps will you take to ensure your passwords and accounts are secure?*
I will make sure to use stronger passwords, enable two-factor authentication where possible, and avoid reusing the same password across multiple accounts.

### *What would you do if you suspected a security breach?*
If I suspected a security issue, I would immediately report it to the appropriate team member or supervisor and avoid interacting with any suspicious activity until the situation is reviewed.

## Task:
I will make sure that my work accounts use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication whenever possible. I will also configure my devices to lock automatically after a few minutes of inactivity. One cybersecurity habit I will follow is always checking links and email senders carefully before opening them.
