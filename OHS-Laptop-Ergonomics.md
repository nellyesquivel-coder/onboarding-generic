# Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) – Laptop Ergonomics

---

# English

## Research & Learn

**What are the risks of using a laptop without an external monitor or keyboard?**  
Using a laptop for long periods without an external monitor or keyboard can cause poor posture because the screen is usually too low. This can lead to neck pain, back discomfort, and wrist strain.

**What ergonomic equipment can improve posture when working on a laptop?**  
Some equipment that can help improve posture includes laptop stands, external keyboards, ergonomic chairs, and an external monitor.

**What adjustments should be made to monitor height, chair position, and desk setup?**  
The screen should be close to eye level, the chair should support the back, and the desk should allow the arms to rest comfortably while typing.

**What daily habits reduce the impact of prolonged laptop use?**  
Taking short breaks, stretching, checking posture regularly, and resting the eyes from the screen can help reduce strain.
