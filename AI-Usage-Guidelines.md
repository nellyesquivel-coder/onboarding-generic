## 1. Task I Improved Using AI

One task I improved was organizing and summarizing the problem requirements before starting the development work, as I often find it difficult to maintain a structured order or get my ideas flowing.
In many cases,  the instructions can be very complex, making it difficult to understand the problem descriptions. You can summarize them by simply asking for a direct explanation of what you need to do or how you can do it. This helps you be more efficient  with your time and development and not get lost in the process.

## 2. Critical Review of the AI Output

AI provides effective answers, but it makes many mistakes if you don't know how to use it or how to ask for something. You can even waste more time trying to use it, which can be overwhelming and disappointing, as it often fails to interpret the reality of the request. If you don't review it, any mistake can be the start of a problem, and if you leave all the work to it, you become 100% dependent, which is not recommended. You must be cautious with what you request and have a margin of discretion and privacy.

## 3. Best Practice I Will Follow When Using AI at Focus Bear

Ask questions about something I don't know how to do without compromising Focus Bear.
Use it as a tool for clarity.
Don't depend on it.
Analyze whether what I have requested is correct.
