## Understanding of Focus Bear's mission and vision

## Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
I believe Focus Bear was created as a response to the overwhelming feeling of not being able to focus and easily understand a task for people with ADHD, helping them feel more secure and comfortable with who they are and what they do.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?
Focus Bear solves negative aspects in its users such as procrastination caused by communication media, social networks, or websites, helping them be more punctual with their activities and daily routines.

## Why do you think this mission is important?
It is important because it helps the user become better, creating habits and reducing time loss in each person, being efficient in jobs and academic studies and keeping a daily record, decreasing stress and overwhelm, making them feel capable.

## How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?
Personally, my interest is to provide a solution to any need that a person may have. In this case, the creation of Focus Bear is not only a good idea but also an idea with awareness and empathy, which is often not practiced. Honestly, the cause is everything,  helping and making them feel better and have better performance gives satisfaction, the satisfaction of the clients.

## Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?
Personally, I identify with it. Sometimes I feel very overwhelmed when organizing my day, and at the end thinking that I did not do something creates dissatisfaction. Even simple tasks can seem difficult; what on average may take 15 minutes can sometimes take longer than expected. I believe that more than just a help app, it becomes a connector of acceptance.
